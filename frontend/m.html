How it works

It answers like a person, because it listens like one.

No menus, no "press one for eligibility". Interrupt it, change your mind, ask the same thing a different way. It follows.

  • It knows UPSY's real rules

    The same underwriting the rest of UPSY runs on: sixty percent academic minimum, family-only co-borrowers, roughly twenty four times monthly income, moratorium through your course plus nine months.

    If it does not know a lender's policy, it says so instead of inventing one. And it will never ask you to read out a PAN, Aadhaar or account number.
